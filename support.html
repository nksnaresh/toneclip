ToneClip Support

How do I set a ringtone?

Make your tone, then tap Share → Use as Ringtone. Settings opens with your new ringtone selected. This needs iOS 26 or later.

I don't see “Use as Ringtone”

Open the Files app › On My iPhone › ToneClip. Long-press the tone, tap Share, then “Use as Ringtone”. Ringtones must be under 30 seconds; the app enforces this for you.

Can I use it as a text tone or alarm?

Yes. Once installed, your tone shows up in Settings › Sounds & Haptics for text tones and other alerts, and in the Clock app under Alarm › Sound.

How do I give one contact their own ringtone?

In Contacts, open the person, tap Edit › Ringtone, and pick your tone.

Why can't I import a song from Apple Music?

Apple Music downloads are copy-protected by Apple, and no app can use them. Songs you bought, voice memos and audio files you own all work.

I bought Pro on another device

Open the app › Settings (gear icon) › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel the yearly subscription

Settings › your name › Subscriptions › ToneClip › Cancel Subscription. Lifetime is a one-time purchase with nothing to cancel.

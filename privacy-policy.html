Privacy Policy

ToneClip · Last updated October 22, 2026

ToneClip is built to work entirely on your iPhone. The short version: we don't collect any data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It doesn't run any server of its own.

Your sounds

Audio files, videos and recordings you bring into the app are processed on your device and stay there. Ringtones you make are saved in the app's folder on your iPhone, which you can see in the Files app. We never upload them.

Microphone and Photos

The microphone is used only while you're recording a sound, and only when you ask it to. Videos are picked through Apple's Photos picker, so the app can only see the videos you choose. It has no access to the rest of your library.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name, email or payment details. The app only learns whether Pro is active on your Apple Account.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
